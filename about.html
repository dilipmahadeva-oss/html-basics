About Us

About Woxsen University

Woxsen University was established with the vision of creating an institution that combines academic rigor with practical application.

Over the years, we have evolved into a premier educational establishment known for our commitment to student success and research excellence.

University Timeline

  1. 2010: Woxsen University founded with initial enrollment of 200 students
  2. 2013: Launched research center and expanded facilities
  3. 2015: Achieved national recognition for educational excellence
  4. 2018: Established international partnerships with leading universities
  5. 2021: Expanded to multiple campuses across the country
  6. 2024: Launched innovative online learning platforms
  7. 2026: Celebrating 16 years of academic excellence

Core Values

  • Excellence: Highest standards in education and research
  • Innovation: Creative thinking and groundbreaking solutions
  • Integrity: Ethical academic and administrative practices
  • Inclusivity: Equal opportunities for all learners
  • Community: Supportive and collaborative environment
