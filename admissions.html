Undergraduate Admissions

Woxsen University offers admissions to meritorious students for its 4-year undergraduate programs based on merit and entrance scores.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board
  • Minimum aggregate score of 60% (50% for SC/ST)
  • Valid scores in national entrance exams
  • No age bar for admission

Application Process

  1. Visit the official admission portal
  2. Create your candidate profile
  3. Fill the application form and upload documents
  4. Pay the application fee
  5. Attend counseling and complete verification
