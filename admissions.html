Undergraduate Admissions

Woxsen University offers admission to meritorious students for its 4-year undergraduate programs. Admissions are based on merit and entrance examination scores.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed 10+2 or equivalent qualification from a recognized board
  • Minimum aggregate score of 60% (50% for SC/ST candidates)
  • Valid scores in national entrance examination (JEE Main/State Board exam)
  • No age bar for admission

Application Process

  1. Visit the official admission portal: www.woxsen.edu/admissions
  2. Register and create your candidate profile
  3. Fill the application form with accurate personal and academic information
  4. Upload scanned copies of relevant documents (Marksheets, ID proof, etc.)
  5. Pay the application fee (₹1,500 via online payment)
  6. Submit and download the confirmation receipt
  7. Wait for merit list announcement and counseling dates
  8. Attend counseling and complete document verification

Postgraduate Admissions

We welcome applications from graduates seeking to pursue advanced studies and specialize in their chosen fields through our postgraduate programs.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognized university with minimum 55% aggregate (50% for SC/ST)
  • Valid scores in entrance examinations (CAT for MBA, GATE for M.Tech, etc.)
  • Work experience may be preferred for certain programs
  • English language proficiency (for international candidates)

Required Documents

Essential Documents:

  1. 10th & 12th standard mark sheets
  2. Bachelor's degree certificate and mark sheets
  3. Entrance exam score card (JEE/CAT/GATE/etc.)
  4. Photo ID proof (Aadhar/Passport/Driving License)
  5. Color passport size photograph
  6. Medical fitness certificate
  7. Character certificate from school/college
  8. Caste certificate (if applicable)

Additional Documents (if applicable):

  • SC/ST certificate from competent authority
  • PWD certificate (if person with disability)
  • Sports certificate (if applying under sports quota)
  • Bonafide student certificate from previous institution
  • NRI affidavit (for NRI candidates)
